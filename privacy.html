Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information Miche ("we", "us") collects when you visit miche-site.vercel.app, sign up for emails or texts, or shop with us, and how we use it. Questions? Email us at miche7488@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your email address and mobile number when you sign up, and your name, shipping address and order details when you buy.
  • Your sign-up choices: whether you agreed to emails and to text messages, and when.
  • Basic site data: pages you visit and the device and browser you use. The site also saves a few settings in your own browser, such as whether you already closed the discount popup or have a code.

Payments are handled by our checkout provider. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

  • To send the emails and text messages you signed up for, like drop alerts, restocks and deals.
  • To process, ship and support your orders.
  • To understand what's working on the site and improve it.

Text messages

If you sign up for texts, we use your mobile number only to send you Miche messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to cancel, or HELP for help. See our text message terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. This does not include the service providers that send our messages on our behalf.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the store, and only for that purpose:

  • Klaviyo, which sends our emails and text messages.
  • Shopify, which runs our checkout and orders.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.
  • Shipping carriers, to deliver your order.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Emails: tap "unsubscribe" at the bottom of any email.
  • Texts: reply STOP to any message.
  • Your data: email miche7488@gmail.com to see, correct or delete the information we have about you.

Keeping it safe

We keep your information with the providers above, which protect it with industry-standard security. We keep it only as long as we need it for the reasons in this policy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
